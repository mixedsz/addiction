Help & Guide

Everything you need to know about creating and configuring drugs

How the System Works

When a player uses a drug item from their inventory, the server removes it, triggers the drug animation, applies screen effects, and starts an addiction timer. The HUD shows immunity and active addictions. Once the timer expires the player enters withdrawal — movement is impaired and a screen effect plays until they take another dose or use the correct medication.

Basic Info Fields

Item NameThe exact inventory item name (e.g. oxycodone_10mg). Must match the item in your ESX items table. Lowercase, no spaces.
Display LabelHuman-readable name shown in notifications and the HUD (e.g. Oxycodone 10mg).
Animationpill – swallowing animation  |  smoke – 10 s smoking scenario  |  syringe – injection with prop  |  sniff – face-palm snorting anim

Drug Strength & Immunity

Drug StrengthHow much this drug drains from the player's immunity pool per active dose. Stack multiple drugs and the total strength adds up.
Drug Immunity(Settings page) The maximum combined strength the player can have before overdosing. Default: 100. If total active strength exceeds this, the ped dies instantly.

Example: DrugImmunity = 100, Drug A strength = 60, Drug B strength = 50 → using both = 110 → overdose.

Health Effects

Armour GainBullet-proof vest armour added when the drug is taken. 0 = none, 100 = full armour.
Health Gain/LossHP added (positive) or removed (negative) immediately on use. 0 = no effect.

Addiction

Chance (%)Probability the player becomes addicted on first use. 0 = never addicted (effects still apply). 100 = always addicted on first use.
Time (minutes)How long after taking the drug before withdrawal starts. The HUD bar shows the countdown. At 0 the player begins suffering until they redose or use the right medication.

Effects

Duration (seconds)How long the visual effects (screen FX, motion blur, speed change, camera shake) last after taking the drug.
Speed MultiplierMovement speed modifier during the effect. <1.0 = slower (e.g. 0.7 = sluggish), >1.0 = faster (e.g. 1.2 = stimulant). Max 1.49.
Walking StyleAn animation clip set applied to the ped's movement. None leaves default walking. Common choices: slightly drunk, moderately drunk, very drunk.
Camera ShakeIntensity of the FAMILY5_DRUG_TRIP_SHAKE camera effect. 0 = none, 1 = moderate, 3 = extreme.

Medications

A medication is an inventory item that, when used, cures the addictions listed under it. Go to the Medications page, create an entry with the item name (e.g. naloxone), and tick which drug addictions it cures. The item must exist in your ESX items table.

Recommended Starting Values

Mild drugStrength 4–6 · Chance 0% · Time 90 min · Duration 20 s · Speed 0.95
Medium drugStrength 8–12 · Chance 25% · Time 60 min · Duration 30 s · Speed 1.1
Strong drugStrength 15–20 · Chance 50% · Time 45 min · Duration 45 s · Speed 0.75
